Agenda

A one-day programme mixing short talks, case discussions and time to meet colleagues. All times are UK time.

  1. 9:00

    Welcome and coffee

    Registration, coffee and a chance to meet the speakers.

  2. 9:30

    How CAR T cells work: a plain-language refresher

    From collecting a patient's T cells to engineering and infusing them.

  3. 10:45

    Managing side effects on the ward

    Spotting and treating cytokine release syndrome and neurotoxicity early.

  4. 12:30

    Lunch and poster session

    Browse research posters from fictional teams across the country.

  5. 13:30

    Beyond blood cancers: solid tumours

    What's holding CAR T back in solid tumours, and what might help.

  6. 15:00

    The patient journey: a case discussion

    A nurse-led look at support before, during and after treatment.

  7. 16:15

    Closing remarks

    Key takeaways and next steps.
